- Prefer dense readable prose and bullets over symbolic encodings.
- Do not output AAAK, compressed JSON, or byte-compression claims.

- Do not remove negations, uncertainty, ownership, dates, file names, IDs, or decision rationale.

- Keep the digest under {{token_budget}} tokens.
- If a fact changes, keep the latest state and mention the superseded state only if it matters.
